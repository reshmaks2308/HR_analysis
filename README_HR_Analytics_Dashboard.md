# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze employee attrition and identify factors associated with employee turnover.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee data to understand **why employees leave an organization**.

The dashboard provides insights into employee attrition across departments, job roles, age groups, job satisfaction, work-life balance, promotion gaps, business travel, performance ratings, and salary bands.

The objective is to identify important patterns in employee turnover and provide data-driven recommendations that can support employee retention and HR decision-making.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- **16.2%** of employees in the analyzed dataset have left the organization.
- Employees who left were younger on average (**33.7 years**) than employees who stayed (**37.6 years**).
- Attrition is highest among employees with low job satisfaction (**23%**) and lowest among highly satisfied employees (**11%**).
- Employees reporting poor work-life balance show approximately **31% attrition**.
- Employees with a performance rating of 4 show approximately **18.1% attrition**.
- Employees with a **6–10 year promotion gap** show the highest attrition at approximately **18.4%**.
- Among employees who left, **Travel_Rarely** represents the largest group.

> **Note:** These findings are observations from the available dataset and should not be interpreted as proof of cause-and-effect relationships.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Conduct regular employee feedback surveys and address key workplace concerns.
- Review workloads, overtime, and flexible-work options to reduce employee stress.
- Provide competitive compensation, recognition, and career opportunities to high-performing employees.
- Create clearer promotion paths and employee development plans.
- Strengthen onboarding, mentoring, training, and early-career development programs.
- Regularly monitor employees experiencing low job satisfaction, poor work-life balance, or long promotion gaps.
- Use HR analytics to identify potential attrition risks and support proactive intervention.
